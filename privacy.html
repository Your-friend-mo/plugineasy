PluginEasy Privacy Policy

Published by PluginEasy Compliance Team • Effective: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: November 23, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

PluginEasy is designed from the ground up on strict data minimization principles. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal names, email addresses, or mobile phone numbers, and never track user location or device identifiers.

What telemetry data does PluginEasy process?

Our content delivery network automatically processes standard HTTP request logs (IP address, user-agent, request timestamp) strictly for DDoS mitigation, load balancing, and bandwidth optimization. These temporary server logs are purged every 24 hours.

2. Cookies, Storage & Third-Party Services

We utilize local session storage solely to maintain live search filtering and installation state during active sessions. Third-party offer networks and analytics providers operate under their own transparent privacy disclosures.

How can users submit privacy compliance inquiries?

Users can contact our data compliance office directly at privacy@plugineasy.com with questions regarding data handling practices or policy details.
